Gondola lighting questions, answered

Gondola shelf lighting is low-voltage LED lighting mounted under or behind retail gondola shelving to illuminate products. Under-shelf LED strips draw the shopper’s eye to each product level, while backlit panels make premium displays glow. TITATEC builds both systems for supermarkets, convenience stores, pharmacies and specialty retail.
